Code of Conduct

Placement Policy & Student Guidelines

Standards and regulatory guidelines governing registration, drive applications, interview etiquette, and offer acceptance.

1. Registration & Profile Completeness

  • All final-year students seeking placement assistance must complete 100% of their online profile, including certified CGPA, 10th and 12th marks, and active phone number.
  • Uploading a verified resume in standard PDF format (under 5MB) is mandatory before submitting any drive application.
  • Any misrepresentation of marks, backlogs, or technical credentials will lead to immediate debarment from the placement season.

2. "One Student, One Job" Policy & Dream Offer Rules

  • Once a student is marked as SELECTED by a corporate recruiter, their profile is marked as placed.
  • Students who receive an initial core or IT service offer under 8 LPA are eligible to apply for "Dream Companies" offering a package of 12 LPA or above (at least 1.5x increment).
  • Withdrawing from an ongoing interview round after shortlisting without prior written authorization from the TPO is strictly prohibited.

3. Attendance & Interview Etiquette

  • Formal western attire is strictly enforced for all on-campus and virtual recruitment rounds.
  • Students must report to test labs 15 minutes prior to scheduled assessment timings with their official college ID card.
  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, or external materials are forbidden during online coding assessments.
Key Timelines
Profile Verification Must be completed within 1 week of registration.
Drive Application Window Closes exactly at 11:59 PM on the declared deadline date.
Offer Acceptance Within 48 hours of official results announcement.
